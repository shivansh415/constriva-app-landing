An AI floor plan you can move with your thumb.

Start from a few answers, not a blank page. Change the rooms, see the preliminary cost follow, and ask an AI engineer about your own project.

Questions, not software

How many bedrooms, which layout, a pool or not. Each answer shapes the plan you see.

Drag, swap, resize

Move a room onto another to swap them. Pull a corner to resize. Distances to the plot boundary stay on screen.

Ask what it would cost

“What would a pool add to my budget?” The answer comes from your project and is marked as preliminary.

See the style first

Pick the styles you like and compare concepts before anything is built.

Good questions to ask

  1. What would another bedroom add?
  2. Which stone suits this facade?
  3. What should I decide this month?
  4. How long does a pool take to build?

Good to know.

Is the AI plan ready for construction?

No. It is a starting point. Licensed engineers prepare and check the drawings used on site.

Are AI costs a quotation?

No. They are preliminary ranges. A quotation comes from an engineer and from suppliers.

Can I edit the plan myself?

Yes. You can move and resize rooms by hand.
