Constriva/The Constriva Store

Building materials quotes, without the running around.

Walk the aisles on your phone, stand in front of the slab, see an indicative price and add it to your trolley. One list goes out. One quotation comes back.

Stand in front of it

Marble, porcelain, timber doors, mixers and paint are shown at a size that makes sense next to a person, with the details that matter for ordering.

Quantities that match your room

Enter the length and width and the app works out how many square metres you need, with an allowance for cuts.

One request, checked first

Your list is checked by Constriva before suppliers see it, so units and quantities are clear.

Compared, then you decide

Nothing is ordered until you accept. You see the quotes together and choose.

What you can look for

  1. Marble, granite and porcelain
  2. Doors, joinery and kitchens
  3. Sanitary ware and mixers
  4. Paint, gypsum and ceilings
  5. Cement, blocks and steel
  6. Lighting, cables and air conditioning

Good to know.

Do I pay in the store?

No. The store makes a list and asks for a quotation. Prices on the shelf are indicative.

Are the pictures the real product?

They are representative images. The quotation names the exact product.

Can suppliers join?

Yes. See the supplier page for how requests and quotes work.
