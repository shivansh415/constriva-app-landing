Constriva/Home care

A home maintenance app that tells you what is wrong.

A drip at midnight, an AC that only blows warm air. Describe it, get a clear first answer, and follow the visit until it is fixed.

Pick what is happening

Leak, electrical, plumbing, AC, cracks, finishes. Choose, say how urgent it is and add a photo.

A first answer in plain words

The AI gives a preliminary view of the risk, what to do right now and the likely next step. An engineer confirms it.

Every request in one list

See what is open, who is assigned and what was done before.

Records that help the next repair

Because the building passport knows the brand and the paint code, the next fix starts with facts.

Good to know.

Is the AI answer final?

No. It is preliminary and an engineer confirms it.

What if it is an emergency?

Use the emergency option in the app. If there is danger to life, call your local emergency number first.

Do I need to have built with Constriva?

You can add a property in the app and ask for care.
