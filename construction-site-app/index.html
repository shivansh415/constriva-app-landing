Constriva/For site teams

A construction site app you can use with one hand.

Dust, sun, gloves and no time. Constriva Team is built for the people on site: few taps, big buttons, and reports you can simply speak.

Check in where you stand

Hold to check in at the site. The app notes the time and place, so the day’s attendance is ready without a paper register.

Speak the daily report

Say who came, what moved and what is stuck. Your words become the daily report, ready to review and submit.

Snags with before and after

Drop a pin, describe the problem, add a photo. When it is fixed, the after photo sits next to the before.

Approvals with a signature

Purchase orders and decisions wait in one place. Sign, stamp and the record is kept with the date.

Who uses it

  1. Site engineers and project managers
  2. Contractors and supervisors
  3. Procurement and approval teams
  4. Maintenance engineers after handover

Good to know.

Is this the same app the owner uses?

No. Owners use the Constriva app. Site teams use Constriva Team. Both work on the same project.

Which languages are available?

English, Arabic, Hindi and Russian.

Does the owner see everything we write?

Owners see approved progress, photos and decisions. Internal approvals stay with the team.
