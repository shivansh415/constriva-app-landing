Constriva/For owners

The home construction app that keeps you in the picture.

Building a house means hundreds of decisions, dozens of people and one budget. Constriva puts all of it on your phone, in plain words.

Progress you can trust

A percentage means little if anyone can type it. In Constriva, progress counts only after the engineer on your project approves it, with photos from the site. You see the stage, the week and what happens next.

Every quote, side by side

Describe what you need once. Verified suppliers reply with priced quotes, and the app lines them up with delivery, fixing and lead time, so you compare like with like.

Decisions with a deadline

Stone, tiles, window colour: each choice arrives as a short card with options and the date it is needed. Tap one and the site knows.

A passport for your home

At handover you keep the as-built drawings, installed brands, paint colours and warranties in one building passport, ready for the next repair or the next owner.

Where to begin

  1. Draw a first plan by answering a few questions, or share the drawings you have.
  2. Ask for a quotation. An engineer reviews it and speaks to you.
  3. Read the agreement and sign with a one-time code.
  4. Follow each phase until you get the keys.

Good to know.

Do I need plans before I start?

No. The app can draw a first layout from your answers and you can move rooms by hand. If you have plans already, share them with the engineer.

Are the costs in the app final?

Early figures are marked as preliminary. Final prices come from supplier quotes for your own quantities.

Can I use it if I live in another country?

Yes. Progress, photos, quotes and decisions reach you on your phone wherever you are.
