Good to know.

I don’t have plans yet. Can I start?

Yes. Answer a few questions and the app draws a first layout you can edit by hand. If you already have plans, upload a PDF, photo or sketch.

Are the prices final?

Early costs are always marked as preliminary. Final prices come from the quotes verified suppliers send for your own quantities.

Who checks the work on site?

The engineer on your project. Progress is counted only after they approve it, with photos.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Arabic, Hindi and Russian.

I’m an engineer or a supplier. Which app is mine?

Engineers, contractors and site teams use the Constriva Team app. Suppliers work in the supplier web portal.
